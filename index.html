How to measure

A · Length

From the highest point of the shoulder, right beside the collar, straight down to the bottom hem.

B · Chest

Around the fullest part of your chest with the tape level. On a tee laid flat, measure straight across from armpit to armpit.

C · Sleeve

From the center back of the collar, along the shoulder seam, then down to the end of the sleeve.

Between sizes? Go up for a relaxed fit, or down for a closer fit.
