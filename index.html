How to measure

Three numbers. No guessing.

A

Length

Start at the highest point of the shoulder, right next to the collar. Run the tape straight down to the bottom hem.

B

Chest & width

Your body: wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest and keep it level. A tee: lay it flat and measure straight across from one armpit seam to the other.

C

Sleeve

Start at the center back of the collar. Follow the shoulder seam out, hold the tape at the shoulder, then continue down to the end of the sleeve.
