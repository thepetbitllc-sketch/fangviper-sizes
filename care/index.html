Product care

Treat your gear right and it keeps showing up, wash after wash. A few small habits keep prints sharp and your gear working like new.

T-Shirts

  • Wash

    Machine wash cold, inside out, on a gentle cycle with mild detergent and similar colors. Use non-chlorine bleach only when necessary. No fabric softener.

  • Dry

    Tumble dry on low, or hang dry for the longest life.

  • Iron

    Cool iron, inside out, only if needed. Never iron directly on the print.

  • Dry clean

    Do not dry clean.

Stainless steel water bottle

  • Hand wash only

    Wash your bottle by hand only.

  • Not dishwasher safe

    Keep it out of the dishwasher to protect the vacuum seal.
